Effective date: May 26, 2026

ShadowCue is a free English shadowing practice app. The first release is built
to work without accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud sync.

## Data Collection

ShadowCue does not collect personal data.

The app does not use:

- Account login
- Third-party analytics
- Advertising SDKs
- Cross-app or cross-website tracking
- Microphone recording
- Cloud sync

## Local Data

ShadowCue stores learning progress, vocabulary marks, review status, and related
practice state locally on the user's device. This data is used only to keep the
learning experience consistent inside the app.

Deleting the app removes this locally stored app data from the device.

## Content

ShadowCue may include bundled learning content in the app package. The app does
not upload user files or learning progress to a server in the current release.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the project support page:

[ShadowCue Support](../support/)

## Changes

If ShadowCue adds account login, analytics, cloud sync, in-app purchases, or
other online services in the future, this policy and the App Store privacy
answers will be updated before that version is released.
